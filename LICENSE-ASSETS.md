## Lettering and typography

Syne and Manrope are locally bundled Latin variable fonts from the Fontsource packages. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included in docs/licenses. EMS Allure is a single-line derivative of Allura (Rob Leuschke, TypeSETit), drawn by Sheldon B. Michaels and converted by Windell H. Oskay. The company-name paths use those centre lines and an original finishing stroke. The signature sequence is a text treatment, separate from the configurable official logo.
